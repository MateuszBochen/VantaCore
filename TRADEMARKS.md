# VantaCore name and logo

The VantaCore **code** is licensed under the GNU AGPL v3 (see `LICENSE` and the
additional terms in `NOTICE`). The **name "VantaCore" and its logos** are not
covered by that license - this page says how they may be used.

## Required: keep the attribution

Every copy and every modified version of VantaCore - including one you only run
as a service for others - must keep the attribution

> Powered by VantaCore - https://github.com/&lt;owner&gt;/vantacore

visible and linked in its user interface (e.g. footer or "About" page). This is
an additional term under section 7(b) of the AGPL, so it is a license condition,
not just a request.

## Allowed without asking

- Running unmodified VantaCore under its own name, for yourself or your company.
- Saying truthfully that your product is "based on VantaCore", "a fork of
  VantaCore" or "compatible with VantaCore".
- Using the name in articles, tutorials, talks and reviews.

## Not allowed without written permission

- Calling a **modified** version "VantaCore" (or a confusingly similar name) as
  if it were the official project - give your fork its own name and keep the
  "Powered by VantaCore" attribution.
- Implying that the VantaCore authors endorse, support or certify your version,
  product or service.
- Using the VantaCore logo as your own product's or company's logo.

Questions or permission requests: open an issue or contact the maintainer.
