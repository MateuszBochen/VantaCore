# Contributing to VantaCore

Thanks for your interest in improving VantaCore!

## Licensing of contributions

VantaCore is licensed under the **GNU AGPL v3 or later** (see `LICENSE`), with
the additional attribution and trademark terms in `NOTICE` and `TRADEMARKS.md`.

Before your first pull request can be merged, you need to agree to the
**Contributor License Agreement** in [`CLA.md`](CLA.md). In short: you keep the
copyright to your work, and you allow the project to distribute it under the
AGPL and also under other licenses (which lets the project offer commercial
licensing).

### How to sign

When you open your first pull request, the CLA bot posts a comment with a link.
Sign there once with your GitHub account - it then covers all your future
contributions. If you contribute on behalf of your employer, make sure you are
allowed to (see section 5 of the CLA).

## Pull requests

- Keep changes focused - one feature or fix per pull request.
- Follow the existing structure and conventions; the backend architecture is
  described in `Api/CLAUDE.md`.
- Describe what you changed and how you tested it.
- Never commit secrets, real credentials or customer data.
